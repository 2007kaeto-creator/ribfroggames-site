Privacy Policy

Endzone Legends · Effective November 20, 2026

This policy explains what information Endzone Legends ("the game"), made by Ribfrog Games LLC ("we", "us"), collects and how it is used.

What we collect

We do not collect personal information. The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising and no analytics or tracking added by us. We don't sell or share any data, because we don't receive any.

Data stored on your device

Your saved games, careers and settings are stored only on your own device (or in your browser, if you play a web version). They are not sent to us. Deleting the game, or clearing your browser's site data, deletes them.

Beta testing with TestFlight

If you test the game through Apple's TestFlight, Apple may share crash reports, basic device information and any feedback you choose to send with us, so we can fix problems. This is handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. We use it only to improve the game.

Contacting us

If you email us, we will see your email address and message and use them only to reply. We don't add you to any mailing list.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If the game starts collecting any information in the future, we will update this page before that happens and change the effective date above.
